# Demo resale estimates

Rough UK resale values for the public founder wardrobe, researched on 23 October 2026. Each new figure is rounded to the nearest £5. Dollar listings are converted at about £0.75 to the dollar. Condition is unknown, so branded pieces are stepped down from a new or asking price where the only comp is unworn.

The four pieces that already had a curated market band keep that range. New wardrobe estimates are a single figure (`valueLow` and `valueHigh` are equal) so both ends of the demo total move by the same amount. The wishlist bangle is stored on its `price` field and is not part of the wardrobe total.

Confidence is high when a sold UK listing matches the piece, med when a live listing or a close comp does, and low when the piece is generic or the comp is only a stand-in.
